01 / About

I make security legible to the people who fund it.

Most of my career has been spent walking into security programs that were unstructured or reactive and turning them into real, measurable operations — the kind that keep running after I leave because the metrics tell the story, not me chasing status.

At IAG — the parent of British Airways, Iberia, and Aer Lingus — I owned vulnerability management and security risk governance across a 160-country hybrid environment. I built the risk-based prioritization, the SLAs, the executive dashboards, and the audit-readiness automation that made the program run.

The thread through all of it is influence without authority: getting engineering, infrastructure, and compliance teams who don't report to me to adopt controls — by speaking their language and removing friction, not issuing mandates. And translating technical risk into business terms a board can actually decide on.

02 / Signature work

Three things I'm known for.

Risk-based vulnerability management

Rebuilt a chaotic 2,000-finding backlog into a prioritized program using CVSS, EPSS, exploitability, and business impact — lifting closure rates 35% and SLA compliance from 60% to 95%.

Continuous audit readiness

Turned a three-week manual evidence scramble across 160 countries into continuous, automated collection — cutting compliance gaps 20% and eliminating missed audit deadlines.

Executive risk translation
